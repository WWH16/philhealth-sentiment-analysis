What happens to your comment

CSAS reads your comment and sorts it as positive, neutral, or negative for the office’s staff dashboard.

sentiment_satisfied_alt

Positive comment

What the office doesRecords it with the staff member who assisted you, so the office can see who was commended.

sentiment_neutral

Neutral comment

What the office doesStaff read it on the dashboard and mark it reviewed once it has been looked at.

sentiment_dissatisfied

Negative comment

What the office doesLists it for staff to follow up, and keeps it open on the dashboard until it is marked resolved.
